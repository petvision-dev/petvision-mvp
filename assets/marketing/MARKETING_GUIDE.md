## 🎨 Brand Guidelines

### Color Palette
- **Primary:** #0df2a6 (Teal Green)
- **Primary Dark:** #16b78f (Dark Teal)
- **Background Dark:** #10221c (Dark Green)
- **Background Light:** #f5f8f7 (Light Mint)
- **Text:** #0d1c17 (Dark Green)
- **Text Light:** #e8f5f1 (Light Mint)


### Typography
- **Headlines:** Inter 800 ExtraBold
- **Subheadlines:** Inter 700 Bold
- **Body:** Inter 500 Medium
- **Captions:** Inter 400 Regular


### Voice & Tone
- **Empathetic:** Understand pet owner worries
- **Confident:** Show expertise and reliability
- **Actionable:** Always provide next steps
- **Warm:** Friendly but professional
- **Direct:** No fluff, get to value quickly

## 🖼 Image Generation Services

### Recommended Tools

1. **Midjourney** (Best for creative/lifestyle)
   - High quality artistic images
   - Good for hero shots and lifestyle photography
   - Discord: https://discord.gg/midjourney


2. **DALL-E 3** (Best for product shots)
   - Great for app screenshots and mockups
   - Integration with ChatGPT Plus
   - Consistent style


3. **Stable Diffusion** (Best for customization)
   - Free and open source
   - Fine-tune to your brand
   - Control every aspect

   - WebUI: https://stablediffusionweb.com


4. **Leonardo.AI** (Best for social media)
   - Pre-optimized for social platforms
   - Fast generation
   - Affordable pricing

   - Website: https://leonardo.ai


### Using the Prompts

1. Navigate to `assets/marketing/prompts/`
2. Copy the prompt you need
3. Paste into your chosen image generator
4. Adjust parameters (aspect ratio, style)
5. Generate multiple variations
6. Select best output

7. Save to `assets/marketing/images/`

## 🔍 Quality Checklist

### Image Quality
- [ ] High resolution (minimum 1080x1080)
- [ ] Brand colors consistent (#0df2a6)
- [ ] App interface visible and legible
- [ ] Professional lighting and composition
- [ ] No distracting elements
- [ ] Text overlay areas clean


### Copy Quality
- [ ] On-brand voice and tone
- [ ] Clear value proposition
- [ ] Grammatically correct
- [ ] No typos or errors
- [ ] Compelling hook in first 2 lines
- [ ] Clear call-to-action

- [ ] Hashtags relevant and researched


### Platform Compliance
- [ ] Correct aspect ratio for platform
- [ ] Text within safe zones
- [ ] File size optimized
- [ ] Alt text included
- [ ] Captions under character limits

## 📊 Tracking Metrics

### Key Metrics to Watch
- **Engagement Rate:** (likes + comments + shares) / followers
- **Click-Through Rate:** link clicks / impressions
- **Conversion Rate:** app downloads / link clicks
- **Sentiment:** positive/negative comment ratio
- **Virality:** shares / total impressions


### A/B Testing Ideas
1. **Hook variations**: Test 3 different opening lines
2. **Image styles**: Lifestyle vs. product shot vs. infographic
3. **CTA variations**: "Download free" vs. "Start tracking" vs. "Try now"
4. **Posting times**: Morning vs. evening vs. weekend
