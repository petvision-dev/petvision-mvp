Frequently Asked Questions

How accurate is the AI?
Our AI is trained on veterinary data and catches patterns you might miss. But it's a starting point for you and your vet—never a replacement. When in doubt, always call your vet.
Can I use this instead of a vet?
No. This helps you communicate better with your vet. Your vet is the expert. We help you be more prepared.
What pets are supported?
Dogs and cats in this version. We're expanding to rabbits, birds, and small animals soon.
Is my pet's data private?
Yes. Your pet's health information is encrypted. You control who sees it. We never sell your data.
Can I track multiple pets?
Yes. You can track multiple pets with completely separate profiles and health histories.
Do I need a credit card?
No. Start free with no credit card required. No hidden fees. Cancel anytime.
